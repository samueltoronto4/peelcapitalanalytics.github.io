About the initiative

Learning finance by solving real problems.

Peel Capital Analytics is a student-led initiative focused on learning finance, business analysis, and entrepreneurship through practical projects with local businesses.

The goal is simple: create useful work while building real-world skills in financial analysis, data visualization, communication, and business strategy.

We provide analytical and educational support, not accounting, tax, investment, or legal advice.
